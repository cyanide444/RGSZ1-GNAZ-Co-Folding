TRANSITION-STATE ANALYSIS: GNAZ.RGSZ1 (rank_1) vs 1AGR (RGS4.GNAI1 TS mimic)
=============================================================================
Date: 2026-07-30
Session analysed : rank1(4contact)_1AGRsuperimposed.cxs (ChimeraX Matchmaker fit)
Author's note    : 1AGR is RGS4.Gai1, not RGSZ1.GNAZ, but the Ga catalytic site is
                   conserved, so it is a valid transition-state reference.

WHAT THE TWO STRUCTURES ARE
  1AGR  = Gai1 . GDP . AlF4- . Mg2+   -> TRANSITION-STATE MIMIC.
          AlF4- is planar and sits where the gamma-phosphate is during hydrolysis;
          an apical water (the nucleophile) completes the Al coordination in-line
          with the leaving group. This is as close to the true TS as crystallography gets.
  rank_1 = GNAZ . GTP . Mg2+          -> GROUND STATE (Michaelis-like).
          Intact tetrahedral gamma-phosphate; AlphaFold3 models no ordered waters.

METHOD
  Structures were superposed in ChimeraX by Matchmaker (Ga vs Ga). Both models were
  written out in the shared (superimposed) coordinate frame (combine -> save; files
  _1agr_super.pdb, _rank1_super.pdb) and all distances below were computed in that
  common frame (script: ts_analysis.py). Arginine finger and catalytic water were
  identified geometrically (closest guanidinium N / closest HOH to AlF4 + Gln amide).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUPERPOSITION QUALITY AT THE ACTIVE SITE  (Ca of equivalent residues)
     catalytic Gln    Gai1 204 / GNAZ 205   Ca-Ca = 0.50 A
     arginine finger  Gai1 178 / GNAZ 179   Ca-Ca = 0.46 A
     Switch I Thr     Gai1 181 / GNAZ 182   Ca-Ca = 0.20 A
   -> the catalytic scaffold of the two proteins overlays to well under 1 A;
      the comparison below is therefore meaningful residue-by-residue.

2. CATALYTIC GLUTAMINE   Gln204 (Gai1, TS)  vs  Gln205 (GNAZ)   [the headline]
   Whole-side-chain overlay after superposition:
     CA 0.50 | CB 0.65 | CG 0.91 | CD 0.85 | OE1 0.71 | NE2 0.94  (Angstrom)
   -> GNAZ Gln205 occupies essentially the SAME position and rotamer as the
      transition-state Gln204, INCLUDING the amide head group (OE1/NE2 within ~0.7-0.9 A).
      The catalytic glutamine is already in its transition-state conformation in the
      ground-state GNAZ.RGSZ1 model - it is pre-organised, not retracted.

3. NUCLEOTIDE / METAL / ARGININE-FINGER GEOMETRY
     gamma-phosphate:  AlF4 Al (TS)  vs  GTP P-gamma (rank_1)   = 0.59 A apart
     catalytic Mg2+ :  Mg (1AGR)     vs  Mg (rank_1)            = 0.29 A apart
     arginine finger:  Gai1 Arg178 guanidinium 3.69 A from Al;
                       GNAZ Arg179 guanidinium 3.39 A from P-gamma
                       (the two guanidinium tips are 1.60 A apart -> both engaged,
                        modest rotamer difference)
   -> the scissile phosphate, the catalytic metal and the arginine finger of the
      GNAZ ground state all sit within ~0.3-0.6 A of their transition-state positions.

4. TRANSITION-STATE PROBE  (place the 1AGR nucleophilic water into the rank_1 frame)
     In 1AGR:   water -> Al = 1.95 A ;  water -> Gln204.NE2 = 3.55 A
     Mapped to rank_1: same water point -> GNAZ Gln205.NE2 = 2.96 A
                                        -> GTP P-gamma      = 2.51 A
   -> if the attacking water sat where it does in the true TS, GNAZ Gln205 would be
      positioned to hydrogen-bond/orient it (2.96 A - even tighter than in 1AGR), and
      it would lie in-line over the gamma-phosphate. GNAZ is geometrically poised to
      position the nucleophile.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
  The AF3 GNAZ.RGSZ1 model, although a ground-state GTP complex, has an active site
  that is strikingly transition-state-like: catalytic Gln205, Mg2+, the gamma-phosphate
  and the Arg179 finger all superimpose on the 1AGR transition-state complex to within
  ~0.3-0.9 A. In particular Gln205 is already in the TS rotamer with its amide aimed at
  the position the nucleophilic water occupies in 1AGR. This is exactly what the RGS
  transition-state-stabilisation mechanism predicts: RGSZ1 binding pre-organises the
  GNAZ switch/catalytic apparatus into the productive (pre-hydrolysis) geometry.

  This supports the mechanistic reading from the interface analysis: the RGSZ1 a4/a7
  contacts that clamp Switch I (around Thr183) hold Thr182 on the Mg2+ and lock Gln205,
  Arg179 and the gamma-phosphate in a catalytically competent arrangement - i.e.
  transition-state stabilisation, not Mg2+ removal.

CAVEATS
  - Ground state, not the TS itself: rank_1 has a tetrahedral GTP gamma-phosphate, not
    the planar/pentacoordinate AlF4- TS. This shows the site is POISED, not the actual
    bond-breaking geometry.
  - No nucleophilic water is present in the AF3 model (AF3 omits ordered solvent); the
    "water probe" borrows the 1AGR water position.
  - Reference is Gai1/RGS4. Side-chain rotamers (Gln205, Arg179) are AF3 predictions.
  - RGSZ1's residue homologous to the RGS4 catalytic Asn128 is a Ser - CONFIRMED against
    canonical human RGS20 (2026-07-31), a genuine divergence. Numbering: AF3 Ser67 =
    docking Ser69 = human Ser328. It does NOT contact GNAZ Gln205 (4.48 A; cf. RGS4
    Asn128->Gai1 Gln204 = 3.04 A H-bond) and instead H-bonds Switch II Glu208 (2.52 A).
    Yet Gln205 is still positioned in the TS rotamer here, so in this pair the catalytic
    Gln is pre-organised by the Ga switch clamp rather than by a direct RGS-Asn contact.
