RGS-DOMAIN SECONDARY-STRUCTURE TOPOLOGY: RGS4 (1AGR) -> RGSZ1
==============================================================
Helix nomenclature (a1-a9) and the two subdomains follow Tesmer, Berman, Gilman &
Sprang, Cell 89:251-261 (1997) - the paper that reported 1AGR. The nine-helix RGS box
folds into a "terminal" subdomain (a1,a2,a3,a8,a9) and a four-helix "bundle" subdomain
(a4,a5,a6,a7). Ga is engaged by three loops of the bundle face: a3-a4, a5-a6, a7-a8.

SOURCES
  RGS4 ranges  : HELIX records deposited with 1AGR (chain E) = authors' assignment.
  RGSZ1 ranges : DSSP (ChimeraX 1.11) on RGSZ1_protein_only_docking.pdb, chain B;
                 short 3-10 continuations merged into the adjacent a-helix.

NOTE on the catalytic residue (a5-a6 loop)
  The near-invariant RGS catalytic asparagine (RGS4 Asn128, which H-bonds the Ga catalytic
  Gln204 at 3.04 A in 1AGR) is a SERINE in RGSZ1/RGS20 (docking Ser69 = AF3 Ser67 =
  human Ser328). This was confirmed against the canonical human RGS20 sequence (2026-07-31)
  - it is a genuine divergence, not a model artifact. In the AF3 GNAZ.RGSZ1 model this Ser
  does not reach GNAZ Gln205 (4.48 A); it instead H-bonds Switch II Glu208 (2.52 A).

NOTE on a1
  The RGSZ1 docking construct spans human ~260-380 (docking file 1-121). At the core
  register (docking file = RGS4 - 59) RGS4 a1 (53-61) maps to human ~253-261, so a1 lies
  almost entirely N-terminal to the construct (which starts at human 260); only its last
  residue(s) are present. DSSP finds no separate a1 helix - the first modeled helix is a2.
  a1 is in the terminal subdomain and makes NO Ga contact, so its truncation removes no
  interface residue, but the construct is not the full RGS box (it lacks a1).

NOTE on register
  docking file = RGS4 - 59 is exact for a3-a9 (verified by both alignment and DSSP overlap).
  a1/a2 carry ~2-residue uncertainty (N-terminal sequence divergence + a1 truncation).
